About WebSutra Webring

What is WebSutra?

A Webring is a circular network of personal websites linked together. It helps people discover handmade amateur websites, personal blogs, and indie projects across India without relying on commercial search engines.

Why Join?

There are many talented webmasters in India running indie blogs, personal digital gardens, and hobby sites. WebSutra brings these sites together in a community directory.

How to Join?

1. Click Join Webring to register your site.
2. Save your Webmaster Secret Key securely.
3. Copy the webring widget code to your homepage footer.
4. Log into your dashboard and click Verify Link to activate your directory listing!
