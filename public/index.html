About WebSutra Webring

WebSutra is an independent webring and personal site directory dedicated to celebrating handmade personal homepages, indie blogs, and passion projects created by webmasters across India.

Unlike modern algorithmic search engines, a Webring links personal websites together in a circular ring or directory structure. By putting the WebSutra widget on your site, visitors can navigate seamlessly across indie sites in the network.
